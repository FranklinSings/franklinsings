About Us

Franklin Sings is dedicated to supporting and enriching the Franklin High School Choir program by providing financial support for all students.

Some of the things we help fund:

  • Music
  • Accompanists
  • Festival fees
  • Travel
  • Robe cleaning
  • Scholarships so that every student can participate in choral events
